שלט שוק — the market sign

You were right: A, B and C were three camera angles on one identity. This is a different world, derived from the audience's own — Israeli street bill-posting and market-stall signage. It earns its place on an Operate surface for one reason: market signage exists to be read in one glance, in sunlight, from three metres, which is exactly the phone-in-a-gap scene this product lives in. Type does the work that hairlines did. Colour owns whole regions instead of marking edges.

The mechanic: the day is a yellow sheet. What you owe today is posted at display scale on a fluorescent field; clearing an item strikes it through in red and the sheet gets shorter. When the sheet is gone, the day is done. Everything else is paper.

Karantina (condensed Hebrew display, 700) carries every name, figure and heading. Heebo carries metadata, labels and controls. Ink on yellow is 14.5:1; paper on red is 5.0:1; muted on paper is 5.3:1. No shadows, no radius, no gradients — depth is overlap, rotation and hatching, the way pasted paper does it.

הדף הצהוב

The day owns a full-bleed fluorescent field, not a widget. Its height is your workload — three items is a tall sheet, zero items is a 4px rule. Cleared work stays visible, struck through in red, until the day rolls over: you can see what you did, not only what is left.

Type is the interface

Karantina at 27–30px carries every lead name and every figure. A market sign never whispers a price in 13px grey. Metadata drops to Heebo 13 and gets out of the way.

The flag replaces the hairline

A 10px yellow tab stuck to the row's inline-start edge means "needs you"; a hatched tab means "drifting". Same vocabulary as the sheet, so one lesson covers both regions — and it survives greyscale, since hatch and solid differ in texture, not hue.

The stamp

Guidance and provenance arrive as rubber stamps, rotated off-axis: לדוגמה on the demo lead, red on things you did. Never a rounded pill, never an emoji.

Desktop · 1440 — register

The sheet becomes a standing column at the inline-start edge rather than a band, so the day never scrolls away while you work the register. Nav moves into the ink masthead; the active item is marked by the yellow rule, the same yellow that means "today" everywhere else.

What it keeps, what it refuses

Product truth held

Six stages, both views, search and filters, demo lead, advisory nudge with an exit, per-row menu as the full non-drag path, ≥44px targets, Hebrew RTL with logical properties. Nothing in the PRD or architecture moved.

Refuses a KPI header

Still no totals-and-conversion strip. The only numbers at display scale are a lead's value and the day's open count — both actionable.

Refuses colour-only meaning

Every stage block carries its Hebrew label; drift is hatching, not a hue; Won carries a border weight as well as red. The board survives greyscale and colour-blindness.

Motion, once

One authored gesture: paper settling. Rows enter with a 0.4° rotation resolving to zero over 180ms; a stage change stamps at 120ms; the struck-through line draws left-to-right in 200ms. Under prefers-reduced-motion everything collapses to an 80ms fade with no rotation.

All names, companies and figures are authored demonstration content, not real customers. This world replaces the Modernist system in docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md rather than extending it — choosing it means a new DESIGN.md, a rewritten tokens.css, and a new PrimeNG preset. The three contrast fixes from the previous round are moot here; this palette was built to pass from the start.
